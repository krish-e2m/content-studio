# Email post playbook

Write like a direct note from a trusted operator — not a blast from a marketing cloud.

## Subject line

- Always start the output with `Subject: ` on its own first line (per format rules).
- Specific > clever. Promise one clear payoff. Avoid ALL CAPS and spam triggers.
- Length that survives mobile truncation (~40–60 characters ideal).

## Body

- Open with the point or a short scene — not "Hope you're well."
- Short paragraphs. One idea per section. Scannable on a phone.
- Newsletter: a few short sections with a clear arc. Broadcast: one focused message.
- Soft CTA at the end that matches the brand (reply, click, forward) — not a hard sell unless the brief asks.

## What good looks like

- Feels personal and useful. Reader knows why this email exists in the first 3 lines.
- Grounded in real brand facts — never invent offers, dates, or links.

## Avoid in email

- Fake intimacy ("Hey friend!") unless the brand voice uses it.
- Long preambles, legal-sounding hedges, or slide-deck bullet dumps.
- Hashtags. Emoji overload. "Don't miss out!!!" urgency theater.
