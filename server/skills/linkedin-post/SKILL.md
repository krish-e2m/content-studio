# LinkedIn post playbook

Write for a professional feed where people skim. Sound like a sharp operator, not a brochure.

## Hooks (first line must earn the scroll)

- Lead with a specific claim, tension, or scene — never a throat-clearing opener.
- Strong patterns: contrarian take, concrete number, short story beat, hard-won lesson, question that implies stakes.
- The first line must make sense alone; do not depend on a title or emoji.

## Structure

- One idea only. If you have two, cut one.
- Short paragraphs (1–3 lines). Blank lines between them for mobile scan.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over abstract marketing language.
- Close with a grounded takeaway or soft CTA that fits the brand — not "What do you think?" unless the voice uses that.

## What good looks like

- Feels written by someone who did the work, not an agency template.
- Specific to this brand's world (customers, product, founder POV) — not interchangeable with any other company.
- No hashtags in the body. No "Excited to announce…" energy unless the brand voice truly uses it.

## Avoid on LinkedIn

- Motivational wallpaper ("Consistency is key", "Hustle harder").
- LinkedIn-ese: synergy, leverage, unlock, elevate, game-changer, "in today's fast-paced world".
- Fake vulnerability or humblebrags.
- Bullet spam that reads like a slide deck unless the format rules ask for a list.
