# Substack post playbook

Write for subscribers who opted in. Earn attention with thinking, not hype.

## Short notes

- Conversational 1–3 paragraphs. Feels like a thoughtful aside, not a thread dump.
- One observation or provocation. Leave room for the reader to think.

## Long-form essays

- Put `Title: ` on its own first line when the format asks for a titled essay.
- Strong opening that stakes a claim or scene; then develop ONE idea across short sections.
- First-person when the brand/founder voice is first-person; otherwise brand "we" consistently.
- Close with a grounded takeaway — not a cliffhanger for its own sake.

## What good looks like

- Sounds like the writer actually believes the argument.
- Specific examples, scenes, or observations over abstract thought-leadership.

## Avoid on Substack

- SEO-blog structure ("In this article we will…").
- Growth-hack CTAs and engagement bait.
- Hashtags. Generic "future of X" essays that could be any newsletter.
- Padding to hit word count — prefer clarity over length.
