# Instagram post playbook

Write for a visual-first feed. Captions support the image or slide story — they don't lecture.

## Voice & density

- Warm, punchy, scannable. Short lines beat dense paragraphs.
- 0–2 emoji only if the brand voice already uses them; never decorate every line.
- Sound like a person texting a sharp friend, not a brand announcement.

## Captions

- Hook in the first ~125 characters (before "more").
- One clear idea or feeling; then a soft CTA or save-worthy takeaway.
- Leave air — Instagram rewards rhythm, not walls of text.

## Carousels

- Slide 1 = the hook / promise. Middle slides = one point each. Last slide = takeaway or CTA.
- Each slide is 1–2 short lines max; no paragraph dumps on a slide.
- Caption can restate the arc in 1–3 sentences after the slide breakdown.

## Reel scripts

- First line stops the thumb (pattern interrupt, bold claim, or vivid scene).
- Spoken lines only — what the creator says aloud. One idea per line.
- At most two light on-screen cues. No timestamps, no stage-direction novels.

## Avoid on Instagram

- Corporate press-release tone.
- Hashtag stuffing in the caption body (hashtags are managed separately).
- "Link in bio" spam unless the brief asks for it.
- Generic wellness / hustle filler that could belong to any creator.
